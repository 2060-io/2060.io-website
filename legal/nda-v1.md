## 1. Purpose

The Discloser operates an online data room containing non-public information about 2060 OÜ, its products, and its financing (the "Data Room"). The Recipient wishes to access the Data Room solely to evaluate a potential investment in, or commercial relationship with, the Discloser (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential Information

**2.1** "Confidential Information" means all information made available to the Recipient in or through the Data Room, and any other information disclosed by the Discloser to the Recipient in connection with the Purpose, in any form, whether or not marked as confidential — including business plans, financial information and projections, capitalization and round terms, product and technical information, security documentation, legal documents, and the identity and content of the documents themselves.

**2.2** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can demonstrate: (a) was or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient without confidentiality obligations before disclosure; (c) was lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations; or (d) was independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Confidential Information.

## 3. Obligations

**3.1** The Recipient shall: (a) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (b) keep it confidential and protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care; (c) not disclose it to any third party except as permitted by Section 3.2; and (d) not copy or reproduce it except as reasonably necessary for the Purpose.

**3.2** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its directors, officers, employees, and professional advisers (its "Representatives") who need to know it for the Purpose, provided they are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement. The Recipient remains responsible for its Representatives' compliance. Where several individuals of the Recipient's organization have been granted access to the Data Room, this Agreement, once signed by one authorized representative, binds the Recipient with respect to all of them.

**3.3** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, or a competent authority, provided it gives the Discloser prompt written notice (where lawful) and discloses only what is required.

**3.4** Access credentials to the Data Room are personal. The Recipient shall not share credentials or grant Data Room access to any person not invited by the Discloser.

## 4. No license, no obligation

**4.1** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No license or other right, express or implied, is granted under this Agreement.

**4.2** Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to proceed with any investment or other transaction, and the Discloser gives no representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information.

## 5. Term

**5.1** This Agreement applies from the Effective Date and remains in force for as long as the Recipient has access to the Data Room and for **three (3) years** thereafter.

**5.2** Upon the Discloser's written request, the Recipient shall cease using the Confidential Information and destroy or delete the copies in its possession, except copies that must be retained under applicable law or bona fide document-retention policies, which remain subject to this Agreement.
